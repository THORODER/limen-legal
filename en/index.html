Privacy Policy

LIMEN analyzes your device storage to help you free up space. All processing of your files is LOCAL: we do not send your files or their content to any server. We only use the Internet to show ads (Google AdMob) and for crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).

1. DATA CONTROLLER App: LIMEN (com.limen.app) Developer: Tool Cosoft Country: Colombia Contact: toolcosoft@gmail.com

2. WHAT WE PROCESS

  • File metadata: name, size, type (MIME), modification date and, for images or videos, dimensions. Used ONLY on your device.
  • System information: storage and memory usage (RAM/cache/swap) to show you metrics. We do not read the content of your files to send it anywhere.

3. PERMISSIONS

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), Android 11+: to analyze and clean files of any type.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 10 or lower): read-only storage access.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: photos, videos and audio.
  • INTERNET: ads (AdMob) and crash reports (Crashlytics). It does not upload your files.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): only if you enable the cleaning reminder.
  • BILLING: purchase of Pro / remove ads. We do not request contacts, SMS, calls, location or microphone.

4. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE Cleaning history (aggregates only: file count, bytes and date), preferences, deferred files and trash (metadata). All local; removed when you uninstall the app.

5. THIRD PARTIES

  • Google AdMob: may collect the advertising ID, IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Firebase Crashlytics: receives crash reports with technical data (model, Android and app version, stack trace). It does not include your files.
  • Google Play Billing: handles the Pro purchase. Payment is processed by Google. We do not sell your files or your data.

6. RETENTION AND SECURITY Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Advertising and crash data are retained according to Google's timelines. The only Internet connections are those of ads and crash reports (HTTPS).

7. MINORS The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data.

8. YOUR RIGHTS We do not create accounts or collect personal data on our own servers. You can delete local data by uninstalling the app and manage ads consent in Settings > Privacy options. To exercise your rights (GDPR/CCPA) write to toolcosoft@gmail.com.

9. CHANGES We may update this policy; the date is shown at the top of the screen.

10. CONTACT toolcosoft@gmail.com
